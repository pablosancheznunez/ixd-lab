# Starter activities

This file records the teaching intent behind the first prototype activities. Each activity is an invitation to practise and reflect, not a validated assessment.

| Lab | Activity | Learning evidence for classroom discussion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wireframing | Assemble a course landing page from low-fidelity components; switch canvas width; export the ordered sketch. | Explain the hierarchy and one responsive design choice. |
| Information architecture | Assign course content cards to learner-facing categories. | Explain why labels and placements support findability. |
| Usability | Review a booking form that gives no post-submit confirmation through a selected heuristic. | Describe an observable issue and a testable improvement. |
| Accessibility | Adjust foreground/background colours and text size; inspect a contrast estimate and preview. | Identify what a contrast check can and cannot establish. |
| Interaction patterns | Compare a status notification with a confirmation dialog after a save action. | Justify feedback proportional to action consequence. |
| User behaviour | Interpret one of two clearly synthetic navigation traces. | Distinguish an observed trace in the exercise from a possible explanation; propose ethical evidence collection. |

## Facilitator prompt

Ask learners to keep three columns in their notes: **what I changed**, **what I expected**, and **what I would need to test**. Encourage comparison between alternatives and avoid treating the prototype's formative prompts as grades.
