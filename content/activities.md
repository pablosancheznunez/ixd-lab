# Starter activities

This file records the teaching intent behind the lab activities. Each activity is an invitation to practise and reflect, not a validated assessment.

| Lab | Activity | Learning evidence for classroom discussion |
| --- | --- | --- |
| Wireframing | Assemble a course landing page from components or a template; reorder; switch canvas width; review the design checks; export the sketch. | Explain the hierarchy and one responsive design choice. |
| Information architecture | Sort ten course pages into learner-facing categories, then compare with a reference structure. | Explain why labels and placements support findability. |
| Usability | Review three interfaces (booking form, course search, password reset) with heuristics and severity ratings; export a findings report. | Describe an observable issue and a testable improvement. |
| Accessibility | Adjust colours and text size; inspect the contrast and a suggested passing colour; name barriers that contrast cannot detect. | Identify what a contrast check can and cannot establish. |
| Interaction patterns | Compare status messages, dialogs, undo, and inline validation; choose a pattern for four situations. | Justify feedback proportional to action consequence. |
| User behaviour | Interpret one of three clearly synthetic traces; write observations and explanations separately; choose research methods. | Distinguish an observed trace in the exercise from a possible explanation; propose ethical evidence collection. |

## Facilitator prompt

Ask learners to keep three columns in their notes: **what I changed**, **what I expected**, and **what I would need to test**. Encourage comparison between alternatives and avoid treating the prototype's formative prompts as grades.
